## 📖 About fario-DK

**fario-DK** is an online bookstore designed for readers of all ages and interests.

Our goal is to create a clean, modern, and user-friendly shopping experience where customers can easily browse books by category, discover new authors, compare titles, and find books that match their interests.

We aim to offer a diverse catalog featuring popular titles, new releases, classics, educational books, and books for children and young adults.

---

## ✨ Our Mission

Our mission is to connect readers with books that educate, entertain, inspire, and encourage imagination.

At **fario-DK**, we believe every reader deserves an easy way to discover their next favorite book.

---

## 👁️ Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted and accessible digital bookstore where readers can explore a large variety of books through a simple, attractive, and organized shopping experience.
